The right document

Every certificate is tied to one SKU row, so you know exactly which product and strength it belongs to.

Three quick steps

  1. Find it.Open the product and find the SKU you want.
  2. Check it.Look at the COA column. If there's a preview, open it.
  3. Ask for more.Need a certificate that isn't shown? Put the SKU in your WhatsApp message and we'll tell you what's available.

What a COA does and doesn't tell you

A certificate describes one batch, tested on one date, for the results printed on it. We only link a certificate when its product name and strength match the listing, and we don't claim anything the document doesn't actually say. If you need the certificate for a specific batch, ask when you order.
