Questions we get asked most

Quick answers on prices, certificates, SKU codes and how ordering works. If yours isn't here, message Kyle on WhatsApp.

Who is Care Peptides?

A small research peptide supplier. You browse the catalog here, then deal directly with Kyle on WhatsApp for quotes, stock and delivery.

Are your products for personal use?

No. Everything we sell is for laboratory research only and is not for human consumption. We don't give dosing or usage advice.

Why are prices shown in pounds and dollars?

We price in pounds sterling. The dollar figure is a conversion for reference; your confirmed quote states the final amount.

Is the listed price final?

It's the starting price per pack. Shipping is added when Kyle confirms your order, and multi-pack orders may be quoted lower.

How do I get a certificate of analysis (COA)?

If a COA is linked for your product, open it from the product page. If not, mention the SKU in your WhatsApp message and we'll tell you what documentation is available.

What does a SKU code like RT10 mean?

The letters identify the product and the number is the strength per vial. For example, RT10 is the 10mg vial pack. Quoting the SKU avoids any mix-ups.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, tap the WhatsApp button, and send the message the site writes for you. Kyle replies with a confirmed quote.

How do member points work?

Points appear in your account area and are calculated using the membership rules shown on the site at the time of your order.

Does shipping count toward points?

No. Shipping is shown separately and points are based on the product amount only.

Can I search by SKU or abbreviation?

Yes. The search box accepts product names, common abbreviations such as BPC or TB, SKU codes and strengths.
