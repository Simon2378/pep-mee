How the catalog is organised

Products are grouped by research area: metabolic, repair and recovery, growth hormone and IGF-1, cognitive, dermal, longevity, and lab supplies. Inside each series you'll find one row per SKU with the vial strength, pack size, price and COA status side by side, so there's no guessing which document belongs to which product.

What we don't do

We supply materials for laboratory research only. We don't give dosing advice, make health or treatment claims, or sell for personal use, and we'll decline orders that ask for it.
