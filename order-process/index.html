How to order

From catalog to confirmed quote in four steps

There's no checkout form and no account needed. You pick what you want, the site turns it into a tidy WhatsApp message, and Kyle replies with the final price and delivery.

Start browsingAsk a question first

The four steps

  1. Find your product.Search by name, abbreviation or SKU code, or browse by research area.
  2. Pick the exact SKU.Each row shows strength, pack size, price and whether a COA is linked. Tap “Add to cart” on the ones you need.
  3. Send your list.Open the cart and tap the WhatsApp button. Your SKUs, quantities and subtotal are written into the message for you.
  4. Get your quote.Kyle confirms stock, shipping and the final total, then arranges payment and dispatch.

Good to know

Prices are per pack

Unless a listing says otherwise, one SKU is a pack of 10 vials.

COAs on request

If a certificate is linked you can view it now; if not, ask and we'll tell you what's available for that batch.

Research use only

We only supply for laboratory research and won't process orders described as being for personal use.

Ordering questions

Do I pay on the website?

No. The website only builds your list. Kyle confirms the final price, stock and delivery on WhatsApp, and payment is arranged there.

Is the price on the site what I'll pay?

It's the starting price per pack. Shipping is added at confirmation, and larger orders can sometimes be quoted lower.

Can I see the COA before ordering?

Yes, where one is linked. Open the product page and check the COA column for your SKU, or ask for it in your message.
