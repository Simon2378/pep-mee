Our standards

How we check our listings

If a page on this site says something about a product, you should be able to trust it. Here's how we keep the catalog accurate, and how to tell us when we've got something wrong.

COAs and qualityRead the guides

One row, one product

Every product page is built from a single catalog source. Each table row holds the SKU, strength, pack size, price and COA link together, so a certificate is never shown against the wrong product. Photos are for illustration; the SKU row is what counts.

What our guides cover

Our guides explain how to read lab paperwork: COA fields, HPLC purity, mass spectrometry, batch numbers and vial formats. They link to their sources. They don't make health, treatment or performance claims, and they don't give dosing advice.

Our rules

Match before we link

A COA is only linked when the product name, strength and batch details line up with the listing.

No filling the gaps

We don't claim a result, test or certification that isn't actually written on the document.

Quotes are confirmed

Listed prices are starting points. Kyle confirms the final price, stock and any extra paperwork for your exact SKU.

Spotted a mistake?

  1. Tell us where.Send the page link and the SKU on WhatsApp.
  2. Tell us what.A sentence on what looks wrong is plenty.
  3. We fix it.We check it against the catalog source and correct the page if needed.
