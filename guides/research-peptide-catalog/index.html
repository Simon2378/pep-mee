How to Find What You Need in Our Peptide Catalog

The Care Peptides catalog lists over 100 product series across more than 200 SKUs. Here's how to get from “I'm looking for…” to the exact pack you need in a few taps.

Published by Care Peptides on 11 Jul 2026 · Last reviewed 5 Nov 2026

Quick answer

Use the search box if you know what you want (it understands names, abbreviations like BPC or TB, SKU codes and strengths). Otherwise browse by research area. Open a product to see every strength in one table with price and COA status, then tap Add to cart on the rows you need.

Key takeaways

  • Search is flexible: Try “BPC”, “RT10” or “10mg”. All of them work.
  • One table per product: Every strength, price and COA link is side by side.
  • The cart writes your message: Your list becomes a ready-to-send WhatsApp message.

Search tips

The search box matches full names (“Tirzepatide”), common short forms (“BPC”, “TB”, “GHK”), SKU codes (“TR20”) and strengths (“50mg”). If you're comparing strengths, search the product name and open its page. All sizes are listed together.

Reading a product page

Each product page has one table with a row per SKU: the SKU code, the specification (strength and pack size), the price in £ and $, and the COA status. Where a certificate is linked you can open the preview right there. Not sure what the codes mean? See our SKU guide.

Building your order list

Tap Add to cart on any row. The cart bar keeps a running subtotal in pounds and dollars. When you're ready, open the cart and tap the WhatsApp button. Your list is written into the message for you, ready to send to Kyle.

Frequently asked questions

Can I search by SKU code?

Yes. Type the code (for example RT10) into the search box and it will take you to that exact listing.

Why do some products show no COA?

We only link certificates we hold for that product. If one isn't shown, ask on WhatsApp and we'll tell you what's available.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. Browsing, searching and building a cart all work without an account.
