How to Order from Care Peptides: A Step-by-Step Guide

Ordering from Care Peptides is done personally over WhatsApp rather than through a checkout page. It's quick, and you get a real person confirming your order. Here's exactly how it works from start to finish.

Published by Care Peptides on 11 Jul 2026 · Last reviewed 5 Nov 2026

Quick answer

Pick your products and add them to the cart, then tap the WhatsApp button to send your list. Kyle replies to confirm stock, shipping and the final total, and arranges payment and dispatch in the same chat. When your parcel arrives, check the vial lot numbers against your COA.

Key takeaways

  • No checkout page: Your order is confirmed personally on WhatsApp.
  • The cart does the typing: SKUs, specs and prices are written into your message.
  • Check on arrival: Match vial labels and lot numbers to your order and COA.

Before you order: a quick checklist

  • You've picked the exact SKU (strength and pack size).
  • You know how many packs you need.
  • You've checked whether a COA is linked, or noted that you'll ask for one.
  • You know your delivery country.

Step 1: Build your cart

On any product page, tap Add to cart on the rows you want. The cart keeps a running subtotal. Prices shown are starting prices per pack; shipping is confirmed separately.

Step 2: Send it on WhatsApp

Open the cart and tap the WhatsApp button. A message opens with your list already written: product names, SKUs, specs and prices. Add anything else you need (a COA request, or a question about quantities) and send it.

Step 3: Get your confirmed quote

Kyle replies to confirm availability, shipping to your country and the final total. Payment and dispatch details are arranged in the same conversation. Nothing is charged through the website.

Step 4: When your order arrives

Check that each vial's product name and strength match your order, that seals are intact, and that the lot numbers match the COA you were given. Store the vials as described in our vial storage guide. If anything doesn't look right, message us with photos.

Our research-use policy

We supply for laboratory research only. We don't provide dosing or usage advice, and we'll decline orders described as being for personal or human use.

Frequently asked questions

Do I pay on the website?

No. The site only builds your order list. Payment is arranged with Kyle on WhatsApp once your quote is confirmed.

Is the website price final?

It's the starting price per pack. Shipping is added at confirmation, and larger orders may be quoted lower.

What if something arrives damaged or doesn't match?

Message Kyle on WhatsApp with your order details and photos, and we'll sort it out.
