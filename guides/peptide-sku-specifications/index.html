Our SKU Codes Explained: Reading Product Codes and Pack Sizes

Every product on Care Peptides has a short SKU code, like RT10, BC5 or CU50. Once you know the pattern, you can read any listing at a glance, and quoting the SKU in your WhatsApp message means there's no chance of getting the wrong strength.

Published by Care Peptides on 11 Jul 2026 · Last reviewed 5 Nov 2026

Quick answer

A SKU is letters + a number. The letters identify the product and the number is the strength per vial. The spec beside it tells you the full pack: 10mg*10Vials means ten vials of 10mg each. Prices are per pack.

Key takeaways

  • Letters = product: e.g. RT is retatrutide, TR is tirzepatide, CU is GHK-Cu.
  • Number = strength: RT10 is the 10mg version; CU50 is 50mg.
  • Price is per pack: Unless the listing says otherwise, a pack is 10 vials.

Anatomy of a SKU

Take RT10: RT is the product (retatrutide) and 10 is the strength (10mg per vial). Likewise TR5 is tirzepatide 5mg, SM2 is semaglutide 2mg, BC5 is BPC-157 5mg and CU50 is GHK-Cu 50mg. Different strengths of the same product share the same letters, which makes them easy to compare in one table.

Reading the specification

The specification column shows exactly what's in a pack:

  • 5mg*10Vials: 10 vials, 5mg in each.
  • 10iu*10Vials: 10 vials measured in international units (used for some products such as growth hormone).
  • 3ml*10Vials: 10 vials of liquid, such as bacteriostatic water.

Blends and combinations

Some listings combine two peptides in one vial and use their own SKU name rather than the letters-plus-number pattern. For example, the BPC-157 + TB-500 blend is listed as Wolverine10, Wolverine20 and Wolverine30. For blends, the spec shows the total amount per vial. If you need the exact split between the two peptides, ask when you order.

Prices and currency

Each SKU shows a price in pounds sterling with a US dollar conversion beside it. That's the starting price for one pack. Shipping is added when your order is confirmed on WhatsApp.

Why quoting the SKU matters

Many products come in several strengths. “Retatrutide” could mean any of nine listings; “RT10” means exactly one. Using the cart does this for you: it writes the SKU, spec and price into your WhatsApp message automatically. See the ordering guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is the price per vial or per pack?

Per pack. Most packs contain 10 vials; the spec column always shows the exact count.

What does IU mean?

International units, a measure of biological activity used for some products instead of milligrams.

Can I order a single vial?

Listings are priced per pack. Ask Kyle on WhatsApp if you have a specific requirement.
